Placement: a standing info box at the bottom of the Role Library, Staff and Home pages, in the slot and style of the existing "Keys and skills live in Settings" box.

Role, skill and org chart settings are your organisation's own configuration and describe tasks assigned to AI staff. They are not statements of professional qualification, employment, or authority to act for your organisation. Your organisation is responsible for how these labels, as you set them, portray it to colleagues and outside parties. See the Licence Terms, section X.
