# ADR-0005: The third-party tool names get no line in this disclaimer

## Context

The Staff and Role Library pages list third-party tools beside the roles and skills: Gmail, AWS, Cloudflare, Notion, iFlyTek, GitHub, Outlook, OneDrive and others. A trademark notice (names belong to their owners; no affiliation or endorsement implied) is a different concern from structure and professional titles, and the brief caps the disclaimer at three to four sentences.

## Options considered

- **Add a trademark line to this disclaimer**: one notice covers the page; the disclaimer grows to five sentences and mixes two concerns.
- **Keep it out, raise it separately**: the disclaimer stays at four sentences; the advisor decides whether the Tools list needs its own notice.

## Recommendation given

Keep it out. It is a trademark notice, not part of the structure and titles message, and it would break the four-sentence ceiling. Raise it in the advisor's packet as a separate one-line item.

## Decision

Accepted as recommended (user, 2026-11-04).

## Consequences

- The disclaimer stays at four sentences plus the Home one-liner.
- The advisor's review packet carries a separate item: whether the Tools list on the Staff and Role Library pages needs a trademark notice, and if so its wording.
- No trademark wording is drafted in this session. If the advisor asks for one, it is a new item with its own record.
