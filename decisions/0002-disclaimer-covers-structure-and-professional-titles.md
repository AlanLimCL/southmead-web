# ADR-0002: The disclaimer guards against two misreadings, structure and professional titles

## Context

The brief asked for a disclaimer against "misrepresentation of divisions". Three readings were live: the departments and org chart read as a real or recommended company structure; the role and skill names (Accountant, Legal Reviewer, Compliance Officer, HR Officer) read as professional qualifications; and "divisions" meaning Southmead's own product lines. The screenshots show the skill labels on the Staff and Role Library pages, which is where the larger exposure sits.

## Options considered

- **Structure only**: matches the word "divisions" literally; leaves the professional-title exposure unaddressed.
- **Both structure and professional titles**: one more sentence; covers the larger risk.
- **Southmead's product lines**: a different disclaimer altogether; no evidence in the brief or the screenshots for it.

## Recommendation given

Both, with the professional-title sentence kept in. The skill labels carry the larger exposure, and the word "divisions" does not reach them on its own. The product-lines reading is dropped.

## Decision

Accepted as recommended (user, 2026-11-04). The product-lines reading is closed.

## Consequences

- Every surviving draft keeps a sentence stating that an AI staff member labelled with a professional title is not a licensed professional.
- That sentence is new wording. It adds no capability claim, so it is a clarification for Alan's word rather than a register amendment, and it is the first line counsel should read.
- Draft D (professional-services guard) stays live as a source for the title sentence; draft A remains the base.
- The structure sentence stays as well: departments and the org chart are the client's own configuration, not a real company and not a recommendation.
