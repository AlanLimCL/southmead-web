# ADR-0006: One identical four-sentence block on the Staff and Role Library pages; English only

## Context

ADR-0001 placed the disclaimer on the Staff and Role Library pages with one line under the Home org chart. The remaining form question was whether the two pages share one block or carry text tailored to each. Language needed no question: the product's screens are English only, and the brand rule reserves Chinese materials for later.

## Options considered

- **One identical block on both pages**: one string for the product team, one text for the advisor; both pages show the same role and skill labels.
- **Tailored text per page**: the Staff page could stress the staff member and its title, the Role Library the templates; two strings to review and maintain.

## Recommendation given

One identical block. The same labels appear on both pages, so the same four sentences fit both, and one wording halves the review and the maintenance.

## Decision

Accepted as recommended (user, 2026-11-04). The user's reason, in their words: "Both pages show the same role and skill labels, so the same four sentences fit both."

## Consequences

- The deliverable is two strings: the four-sentence block (Staff page and Role Library page) and the Home one-liner.
- English only. A Chinese rendering is a new item when the product ships a Chinese UI.
- The interview is complete. The recap, the crafted disclaimer and the advisor's packet follow for confirmation.
